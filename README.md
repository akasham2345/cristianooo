🎣 **ORIGINAL FISHING GAME** 🎣

A Python-based fishing game with originality, depth, and personality!

## Features

### 🌟 Core Gameplay
- **Dynamic Fishing System**: Multiple fish species with unique behaviors and personalities
- **Skill Progression**: Level up from 1-10+ to unlock new abilities and improve catch rates
- **Season & Weather System**: Different fish appear in different seasons; weather affects fishing difficulty
- **Realistic Mechanics**: Fish have varying difficulty, weight ranges, and behavior patterns

### 🎯 Original Elements
- **Fish Personalities**: Each fish has a unique personality ("Fierce and calculating", "Shy and intelligent", etc.)
- **Reaction Mini-Games**: Quick-time events make catching challenging fish more exciting
- **Fish Encyclopedia**: Learn about each species, their habits, and your personal catch statistics
- **Achievement System**: Unlock achievements like "Legendary Hunter", "Weight Champion", "Collector"
- **Dynamic Difficulty**: Gear quality, skill level, weather, and fish behavior all affect catch probability

### 💰 Progression System
- **Experience & Leveling**: Gain XP from catches to level up
- **Money System**: Sell catches for money, upgrade gear
- **Gear Quality**: Better equipment increases catch rate
- **Fishing Streak**: Maintain consecutive successful catches for bonuses

### 🐟 Fish Species (8 Total)
1. **Bass** - Common, aggressive fighter (Summer)
2. **Trout** - Uncommon, cautious predator (Spring)
3. **Salmon** - Rare, acrobatic jumper (Fall)
4. **Catfish** - Uncommon, lazy bottom-dweller (Summer)
5. **Pike** - Rare, fierce apex predator (Fall)
6. **Goldfish** - Common, playful escape artist (Spring)
7. **Piranha** - LEGENDARY, dangerous creature (Summer)
8. **Koi** - LEGENDARY, ancient wise fish (Spring)

## How to Play

```bash
python fishing_game.py
```

### Controls
- **[F]ish** - Cast your line and attempt to catch a fish
- **[S]tats** - View your player statistics and achievements
- **[G]uide** - View fish encyclopedia with all species info
- **[Q]uit** - Exit the game

## Gameplay Strategy

### Tips for Success
1. **Pay Attention to Seasons**: Some fish only appear in specific seasons
2. **Check the Weather**: Rainy weather is best for fishing; storms are challenging
3. **Level Up**: Higher skill levels increase your catch rate significantly
4. **Master the Mini-Game**: Quick reflexes help you catch difficult fish
5. **Complete Your Collection**: Catch all 8 fish species for the "Collector" achievement

### Difficulty Scaling
- Fish range from difficulty 1 (Goldfish) to 9 (Piranha)
- Legendary fish require high skill levels to catch reliably
- Weather conditions modify your effective skill level
